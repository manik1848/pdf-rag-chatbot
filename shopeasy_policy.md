## 3. DELIVERY POLICY

### 3.1 Delivery Zones and Timelines

| Zone | Cities | Standard Delivery | Express Delivery |
|------|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| Metro | Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad | 2-4 business days | Next day (order before 2 PM) |
| Tier 1 | Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow, etc. | 4-6 business days | 2-3 business days |
| Tier 2 | Smaller cities and towns | 6-8 business days | 4-5 business days |
| Remote | Rural and remote areas | 8-12 business days | Not available |

### 3.2 Delivery Charges

**Standard Delivery:**
- FREE for orders above ₹499
- ₹49 for orders below ₹499

**Express Delivery:**
- ₹99 for orders up to ₹1,000
- ₹149 for orders ₹1,000 - ₹5,000
- FREE for orders above ₹5,000

**Special Items:**
- Large appliances: ₹199 - ₹499 (includes installation)
- Furniture: ₹299 - ₹999 (based on size and location)

### 3.3 Delivery Process
1. Order confirmation email sent immediately
2. Dispatch notification with tracking number within 24-48 hours
3. Real-time tracking available on app and website
4. SMS/WhatsApp updates at each milestone
5. Delivery OTP required for high-value orders (above ₹5,000)

### 3.4 Failed Delivery Attempts
- 3 delivery attempts will be made
- Customer will be contacted before each attempt
- After 3 failed attempts, order will be returned to warehouse
- Refund processed within 5-7 business days for prepaid orders
- Re-delivery charges may apply for subsequent attempts

### 3.5 Contactless Delivery
- Available upon request during checkout
- Package left at doorstep with photo confirmation
- OTP verification done via phone call

## 4. RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

### 4.1 Return Window

| Product Category | Return Window |
|-----------------|---------------|
| Electronics | 10 days |
| Fashion & Apparel | 15 days |
| Books & Stationery | 10 days |
| Home & Kitchen | 15 days |
| Groceries & Food | Non-returnable |
| Personal Care & Hygiene | Non-returnable |
| Customized Products | Non-returnable |
| Innerwear & Swimwear | Non-returnable |

### 4.2 Return Conditions
Products must be:
- Unused and in original condition
- In original packaging with all tags attached
- Accompanied by invoice/receipt
- Free from damage caused by customer

### 4.3 Return Process
1. Initiate return request from Orders section
2. Select reason for return
3. Choose pickup slot or self-ship option
4. Pack the item securely
5. Hand over to pickup executive (with OTP verification)
6. Quality check completed within 2-3 days of pickup

### 4.4 Refund Methods and Timelines

| Payment Method | Refund Method | Timeline |
|---------------|---------------|----------|
| Credit/Debit Card | Original card | 5-7 business days |
| UPI | Original UPI ID | 2-4 business days |
| Net Banking | Bank account | 5-7 business days |
| Wallet | ShopEasy Wallet | Instant |
| Cash on Delivery | Bank account/Wallet | 5-7 business days |

### 4.5 Replacement Policy
- Replacement available for defective or wrong products
- Subject to stock availability
- If replacement unavailable, full refund will be processed
- No additional charges for replacement delivery

### 4.6 Cancellation Policy
- Orders can be cancelled before dispatch at no charge
- Cancellation after dispatch not allowed; please refuse delivery or return
- Refund for cancelled orders: 3-5 business days
